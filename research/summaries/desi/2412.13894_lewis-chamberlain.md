# Executive summary
Lewis and Chamberlain ask whether the apparent preference for evolving dark energy in recent BAO analyses can be realised by any physical model. Their lever is the null energy condition (NEC): a non-interacting dark energy fluid must have w >= -1, so its density cannot fall with redshift; a cosmological constant saturates this bound. Combined with Planck's measurement of the CMB angular acoustic scale theta_*, which fixes the comoving distance to last scattering given standard early-universe physics, this gives a one-sided integral constraint. They derive strict inequalities on the BAO observables D_H, D_M, D_V and F_AP relative to LCDM: D_M(z) >= D_M^Lambda(z) at all redshifts, D_H starts above LCDM and crosses below it once, F_AP >= F_AP^Lambda, and H0 can only be lower, so NEC-consistent dark energy can only worsen the Hubble tension. Mapping the CPL (w0, wa) plane, they find the line wa ~ -4(1+w0) below which the sign of deviation is wrong at every redshift. Applied to DESI DR2 BAO data, four redshift bins sit inside the excluded region and the best-fit CPL model (w0 = -0.50, wa = -1.47) lies in the forbidden region for z > 0.25. They conclude acoustic data favour LCDM over other NEC-consistent models up to statistical fluctuations, with any thawing preference driven by supernovae. Left open: combining with growth of structure, and what NEC-forbidden tensions would mean. (Summariser context, not from the paper: the frontmatter date is the arXiv v1 date; the PDF summarised is v3, 2025-04-23, using DESI DR2.)

## Claims
- claim: Under the null energy condition and a fixed CMB acoustic scale, certain directions of deviation from LCDM in the BAO observables are impossible for any physical non-interacting dark energy model.
  quote: "These inequalities demonstrate that certain deviations from ΛCDM are impossible for any physical non-interacting dark energy model that respects the null energy condition within the context of FRW cosmological models."
  loc: "Abstract"
- claim: Because D_M(z) >= D_M^Lambda(z) at all redshifts at fixed theta_*, any NEC-consistent dark energy lowers the inferred H0 and so can only make the Hubble tension worse, not better.
  quote: "this is now sufficient to show that null-energy consistent dark energy can only exacerbate the Hubble tension."
  loc: "Sec. II.B.1"
- claim: A CPL (w0, wa) fit over a finite low-redshift range can naively indicate phantom behaviour at high redshift even when the underlying model respects the NEC, so (w0, wa) contours must not be read as evidence of NEC violation without the integral theta_* constraint.
  quote: "It is possible for a model to satisfy the null-energy condition, but, when constrained by measurements over a finite low-redshift range, to give constraints in the (w0, wa) plane that naively imply a violation of the null-energy condition (phantom behaviour) at high redshift"
  loc: "Sec. I"
- claim: The line of constant H0 through the LCDM point, approximately wa = -4(1+w0), divides the CPL plane into a region where the predicted sign of the BAO deviation can be NEC-consistent at some redshift and a region where it is consistent at no redshift.
  quote: "For currently measured parameter values, this line approximately corresponds to wa ≈ −4(1 + w0), though it is not exactly a straight line."
  loc: "Sec. III"
- claim: Four of the DESI redshift bins (LRG2, LRG3+ELG1, ELG2 and Lya) have central values inside the NEC exclusion region, with D_M below the LCDM prediction.
  quote: "For four of the redshift bins – LRG2, LRG3+ELG1, ELG2, and Lya – the data point centres lie within the exclusion region."
  loc: "Sec. IV"
- claim: Current acoustic-scale data favour LCDM over other NEC-consistent models up to statistical fluctuations, because the roughly 2-sigma tensions with LCDM lie primarily in directions that would require NEC violation.
  quote: "Current acoustic data favour ΛCDM over other NEC-consistent models up to statistical fluctuations."
  loc: "Sec. V"

## Methods
- Analytic derivation: flat FRW, non-interacting dark energy, fixed non-dark-energy parameters (theta_*, Omega_m h^2, Omega_b h^2) so that D_M(z_*) is fixed; the NEC (d rho_de/dz >= 0) plus the Friedmann equation yields the inequalities of Table I on D_H, D_M, D_V and F_AP relative to LCDM.
- CPL mapping: for each (w0, wa) at fixed theta_*, compute the sign of the predicted deviation of D_M (and D_V) from LCDM at each redshift and draw the consistency lines in the (w0, wa) plane (Fig. 2).
- Data comparison: plot DESI BAO points in the D_H/D_H^Lambda vs D_M/D_M^Lambda and D_V vs F_AP planes against the exclusion regions, using a Planck PR3 best-fit LCDM reference (Fig. 3) and a Planck early-LCDM posterior to show parameter-shift freedom (Fig. 7).
- Parameter inference with Cobaya, camb and GetDist, using a Gaussian early-LCDM Planck NPIPE likelihood on theta_*, Omega_c h^2, Omega_b h^2, DESI BAO likelihoods per redshift, and optionally Pantheon+, Union3 or DES Y5 supernovae.
- Toy NEC-consistent model w(a) = max(w0 + wa(1-a), -1) ("Max-w0waCDM") to show how constraints change when NEC violation is forbidden (Fig. 6).

## Data
- DESI DR2 BAO measurements at seven effective redshifts, 0.295 <= z_eff <= 2.33: D_M/r_drag and D_H/r_drag with their covariance in six bins (LRG1 z=0.51, LRG2 z=0.706, LRG3+ELG1 z=0.934, ELG2 z=1.321, QSO z=1.484, Lya z=2.33) plus D_V/r_drag only for BGS (z=0.295); Sec. I phrases the range loosely as 0.2 <~ z <~ 2.
- Planck 2018 PR3 best-fit parameters as the fiducial LCDM reference, and Planck PR4 (NPIPE) early-LCDM constraints on theta_*, Omega_c h^2, Omega_b h^2 for the chains.
- Type Ia supernova compilations Pantheon+, Union3 and DES Y5, added one at a time in the joint CPL fits.

## Limitations
- limit: The analysis is purely geometric (acoustic standard ruler) and does not include growth-of-structure or other probes.
  quote: "While we have focused here on geometric constraints from the acoustic standard ruler, a complete picture will require combining these results with constraints from structure growth and other cosmological probes."
  loc: "Sec. V"
- limit: The authors stress that the CPL parameterization they interpret is itself unphysical, so results expressed in (w0, wa) need careful interpretation.
  quote: "Although the CPL parameterization is convenient and easy to implement, it is fundamentally unphysical, so results using it must be interpreted with care."
  loc: "Sec. V"
- limit: The whole argument rests on assuming the null energy condition holds for the background, and the paper acknowledges there is no proof that it must.
  quote: "Although there is no clear reason that the null-energy condition cannot be violated at the level of the background cosmology"
  loc: "Sec. V"
- limit: Part of the roughly two-sigma offset of the joint posterior into the excluded region is attributed to a parameter-volume effect rather than data, and the Max-w0waCDM constraints depend strongly on the prior ranges.
  quote: "though some of this is likely a parameter volume effect"
  loc: "Sec. IV"

## Open questions
- How to robustly identify physically consistent deviations from LCDM when acoustic-scale constraints are combined with other observables such as structure growth (Sec. V).
- If future BAO data show tensions in NEC-forbidden directions, whether this signals measurement systematics or more radical changes such as interacting dark energy, modified gravity or altered early-universe physics (Sec. V).
- Whether the supernova-driven pull towards NEC-consistent thawing at z < 0.2 is physical, given systematic differences between Pantheon+, Union3 and DES Y5 and the fact that it would worsen the distance-ladder tension (Sec. IV and Sec. V).
